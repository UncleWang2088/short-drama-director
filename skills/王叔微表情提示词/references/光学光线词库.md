## 光线质感的描述词汇

### 硬光与软光
- 硬光: harsh/锐利/sharp/crisp/directional/强烈的
- 软光: soft/diffuse/柔和的/漫射的/wrapped

### 光线氛围
- 暖色笼罩: bathed in warm amber light
- 冷色漫射: cold blue light spills across
- 斜光: slanting at 45 angle through ornate wooden grilles
- 残光: dying light / fading glow
- 穿透: light cuts through / pierces the darkness
- 漫反射: diffused illumination bouncing off the dark hardwood walls
